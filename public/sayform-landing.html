What differentiates us in the era of AI?

The way we communicate.
How we explain what we believe, reason on our feet, make sense of a difficult question, and help someone see what we see.

Executive coaching often comes with a price that puts it out of reach. Self-paced courses let you learn on your own, but leave you without someone who listens, gives specific feedback, and asks the question you didn’t see coming.

Sayform was created by people who know what it feels like to be good at your job and still feel like you’re not enough. People who have something to say but struggle to get it out.

Finding your voice takes practice, encouragement, and someone who pays attention.

Sayform puts human coaches at the center and makes coaching more accessible. AI helps them understand your progress, shape your practice, and take care of the paperwork.

Our goal is simple: give more people the support to speak with clarity and confidence, especially when it matters.
